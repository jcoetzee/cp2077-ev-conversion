The year is 2077. Why is every car in Night City still burning fuel?

EV Conversion turns the cars and motorcycles of Night City into electric vehicles, from your starter Archer Hella to traffic, police cruisers and hypercars. Each vehicle keeps its own character, and the conversion is applied to all of them the same way, so no ride gets an advantage.

Still burning CHOOH2: Nomad vehicles (the Aldecaldos' Badlands rigs, including the "Rattler", "Bandit" and "Samum") and Johnny's Porsche 911 Turbo keep their combustion engines, sound and handling exactly as in vanilla. Head out to the Badlands and you'll hear the difference.

What changes

  • No gear shifts. Every vehicle accelerates in one continuous sweep, with no shift pauses and no shift sounds.
  • EV torque. Strong pull from a standstill, then power that tapers off at speed, like an electric motor. Each engine gets its own torque curve, fitted to its original performance.
  • Battery weight. +10% mass (assuming 2077-era batteries) with a lower centre of gravity, so cars feel more planted. Brake force and tyre grip are scaled to match the extra weight.
  • Quicker response. Snappier throttle, and lifting off slows the car more, like regenerative braking.
  • Electric sound. Every converted vehicle's engine sound is replaced with the hum of the Militech Basilisk hover tank. Brakes, tyres, horn, doors and radio keep their original sounds. Traffic is almost silent apart from tyre and road noise, like real EVs in the city.

What stays the same

  • 0–100 km/h (as shown on the speedometer) matches vanilla. The calibration was checked with stopwatch runs: Archer Hella 8.59 s vs 8.70 s in vanilla, Sport R-7 "Vigilante" 4.42 s vs 4.32 s.
  • Acceleration to 80% of top speed matches vanilla.
  • Top speed, drivetrain (FWD/RWD/AWD), cornering grip, steering, suspension and the reverse gear are unchanged.

Important

  • Fully restart the game after enabling or disabling the mod. Toggling it from the main menu without restarting leaves cars that won't move or won't stop.
  • Conflicts: this mod replaces the game's vehicle sound settings file (cooked_metadata.audio_metadata). Other mods that replace the same file won't work alongside it.
  • Built for Update 2.5. One file for all platforms. Playtested on macOS.
  • The mod only changes vehicle data, not your save.

Known quirks

  • Some sports cars, such as the Sport R-7, still show exhaust flashes as a visual effect.
  • Shared engines: a few vehicle families use one engine across body styles of very different weights, such as vans and large sedans. Those can be up to about 6% off vanilla acceleration.

Open source

The build scripts, calibration data and format notes are on GitHub under the MIT licence: github.com/jcoetzee/cp2077-ev-conversion. Contributions are welcome, especially a real EV motor sound.
